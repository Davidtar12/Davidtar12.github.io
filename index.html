about

How I work.

The throughline in everything I've done is the same: take something complex and find the version of it that makes someone stop. In investigative journalism, that's the lede that makes you read the whole piece. In financial content, it's the one-sentence insight that explains what the Fed just did to someone who doesn't follow the Fed. On TikTok, it's the hook in the first two seconds.

The platform changes. The instinct doesn't. What has changed is the production infrastructure around it. I'm AI-native by necessity — I run content operations solo that used to require teams, because I've built the systems that make it possible. Multi-agent research-to-publication pipelines. Prompt libraries. Editorial automation that handles distribution so I can focus on what the machine can't do: judgment about what actually matters.

I'm bilingual by origin, not by translation layer. Spanish-native, C1 English (TOEFL iBT 108). I produce content in both languages from the source — which means it sounds like it was written that way, because it was.
